# TrialVault Use Cases

## Oncology Trial

Patients in oncology studies often contribute sensitive genetic markers, diagnoses, treatment history, and response data. TrialVault keeps patient-level values encrypted while allowing researchers to compute response rate, adverse event distribution, and adherence statistics.

Outcome:
- Patients control disclosure of personal results and consent records.
- Researchers receive population-level analytics without patient identity exposure.
- Pharma sponsors preserve an integrity trail for FDA submission.
- Regulators verify completeness and audit events without unnecessary PII.

## Cardiovascular Drug Trial

Baseline blood pressure, heart rate, age, and medication compliance can identify participants when combined with outside data. TrialVault encrypts those values and supports aggregate FHE computations such as average compliance and adverse-event severity.

Outcome:
- Research teams can evaluate safety trends without raw patient rows.
- Trial sponsors can prove the submitted dataset matches committed on-chain records.
- Auditors can inspect access grants, timestamps, and computation receipts.

## Multi-Site Research Network

Clinical trials often span hospitals, CROs, and research sites. TrialVault gives each site a shared integrity layer while preserving local privacy boundaries.

Outcome:
- Sites submit encrypted cohorts.
- Aggregate statistics are computed without pooling raw records in one database.
- Access history remains tamper-evident across institutions.
